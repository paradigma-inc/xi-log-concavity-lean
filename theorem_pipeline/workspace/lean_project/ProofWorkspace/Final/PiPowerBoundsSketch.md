# Rational bounds for powers of pi

The proved Machin interval and rational logarithm interval theorem enclose actual $\log\pi$. Multiplying the two endpoints by a rational exponent $r$ and taking their minimum/maximum handles either sign. The proved range-reduced exponential evaluator then encloses $\exp(r\log\pi)=\pi^r$.

The endpoints are rational. The only extra preconditions are positive exponential orders and explicit rational scaled-range inequalities. This supplies the actual irrational-base factor needed by the Xi grid evaluator; it does not treat pi as an exact decimal constant.

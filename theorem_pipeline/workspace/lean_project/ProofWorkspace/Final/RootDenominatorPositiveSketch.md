# RootDenominatorPositive

For every retained-grid index $k\ne40$, the rational lower bound for $|1-2^{1-s_k}|$ is strictly positive. Here $s_k=(k+40)/80$. Rounded powers of a nonnegative base at most one are antitone in their exponent. Two ordinary-kernel rational comparisons at the nearest indices $39$ and $41$ therefore imply the claim on the entire grid, not just a finite prefix. The removable index $k=40$ is deliberately excluded and handled separately. The proof does not assume any unverified sample table.

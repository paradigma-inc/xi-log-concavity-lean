# Integer evaluation of the certified root-based eta intervals

The root table stores exact integer numerators over $B=10^{180}$. Convert its rounded powers to the proved integer recurrence. For a signed rational Euler weight $w$, multiplication and min/max selection reduce to integer numerators divided by $w.den$. Summing the integer terms and dividing once by $B$ gives exactly the accepted root-based eta endpoints, including the directed $2^{-N}$ remainder.
